## Intent

Make the evidence rule actually hold, permanently.

The captain asked what we are doing about this permanently, after the same failure twice today: two lanes published "2 of 3 scenarios **driven live**" and "0 of 5 / 2 of 3" for runs that were in fact **synthetic or offline built-CLI validation** against fixtures, and firstmate relayed one of those labels to the captain without reading the artifact it named.

The gate we built for exactly this - the evidence-provenance section injected into every brief by `bin/fm-brief.sh` - is real, is present in live briefs, and is **prompt text only**. It tells a worker what to write and nothing checks it, and it does not govern the published pull-request body at all: `bin/fm-dod-lib.sh`, the single owner of what a worker may pass as `--intent` and therefore of the body a pipeline publishes, says nothing about evidence claims. That is the gap this task closes. Do it inside the owners that already exist; do not invent a review process.

## What Changed

- Added mechanical evidence-claim validation requiring artifact, command, and capture-time metadata within approved paths.
- Enforced validation during worker spawn, promotion, and published PR/MR checks, including complete Gerrit descriptions.
- Expanded evidence guidance and coverage for provenance enforcement, path safety, timestamps, and claim detection.
